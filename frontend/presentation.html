Rung 1: Linear Regression

The simplest baseline: one straight line fitted to the training years by least squares.

Model

y(t)= β0+β1t

t counts years from March 1958.

Fitted

β0 = ppm
the concentration at the reference time t = 0
β1 = ppm per year
a constant growth rate

Linear in time, and linear in the two unknown parameters.

Phenomenological Foundation

The Intuition: "The Child on an Escalator"

Atmospheric carbon dioxide measurements at Mauna Loa exhibit two superimposed physical mechanisms: an accelerating anthropogenic secular trend combined with a harmonic biospheric oscillation.

Conceptual Mechanics Schema
Figure A.1
1. The Escalator (Trend)

Relentless long-term fossil fuel consumption and land use change forces the baseline upward at ~2.8 ppm/year.

2. The Jump (Seasonality)

Northern Hemisphere forests inhale CO₂ during boreal summer (-3.5 ppm) and exhale during winter decay (+3.5 ppm).

north_east The Mathematical Synthesis

When a child jumps repeatedly while riding an upward escalator, their altitude constantly oscillates, but each successive floor bounce lands higher than the last. In atmospheric chemistry:

CO₂(t) = Baseline(t) + Seasonal(t) + ε(t)

Where Baseline(t) is an accelerated convex trajectory, and Seasonal(t) is a 12-month cyclic Fourier series with near-zero zero-sum annual net effect.

Key Climatological Takeaways
  • check_circle The annual drop in late summer is temporary biospheric respiration, not emission reduction.
  • check_circle Linear models completely fail over long time scales because planetary fossil fuel combustion compounding is nonlinear.
  • check_circle The seasonal amplitude itself is widening slightly over decades due to Arctic greening and agricultural intensification.

Statistical Framework

Mathematical Formulations & Model Benchmark

Rigorous comparison of mathematical models calibrated strictly against historical training data (1958–2015) and evaluated on subsequent real-world readings.

Baseline Model -14.84 ppm err

1. Linear Fit

y(t) = a + b·t

Presumes a constant annual emission accumulation rate over time. Fails to incorporate biospheric seasonal oscillations and severely underpredicts modern CO₂ concentrations due to post-1970 global emission acceleration.

Held-out RMSE: 12.41 ppm
Sep 2026 Calc: 411.23 ppm
2050 Forecast: 456.12 ppm
Quadratic + Harmonics -2.21 ppm err

2. Polynomial + Seasonal

y(t) = a + b·t + c·t² + S(t)

Incorporates quadratic term for secular acceleration alongside 12-month harmonic Fourier seasonal cycle S(t). Captures annual plant respiration swings but starts falling slightly behind recent decadal surges.

Held-out RMSE: 1.84 ppm
Sep 2026 Calc: 423.86 ppm
2050 Forecast: 482.40 ppm
Best Calibration +0.62 ppm err

3. Exponential + Seasonal

y(t) = a · e^(k·t) + S(t)

Compounds growth proportionally with cumulative carbon emissions. Reproduces both the empirical curvature and harmonic peak/trough cycle with superior generalization over a 10-year blind held-out evaluation window.

Held-out RMSE: 0.76 ppm
Sep 2026 Calc: 426.69 ppm
2050 Forecast: 498.92 ppm

Scientific Assumption Notice

Forecasts assume historical anthropogenic and biospheric dynamics continue without drastic systemic shifts.
